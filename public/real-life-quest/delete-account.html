Super TaskerPrivacy

Request account deletion

For Super Tasker by NomoLabs, you can request account and associated data deletion even if the app is no longer installed.

In the app

The primary parent can open Privacy & Safety and choose Delete this entire family account. Confirm with your password or linked Google account and the confirmation text. This deletes the family's profiles, missions, submissions/photos, reward balances and associated history. A co-parent can delete their own account from Family & account; the rest of the family remains.

Without the app

Email support@nomolabs.pro with the subject Super Tasker account deletion. Include your parent account email and whether you want your own co-parent account or the entire family deleted. We will verify account ownership before processing your request. Do not send passwords, child PINs, identity documents or children's photos by email.

Request deletion by email

Associated live-service account and family data will be removed as applicable. Any necessary security/legal retention will be explained in our response. Device-only progress is managed on your device; contact us for safe removal instructions.
