Privacy policy

Updated 10 November 2026. Real Life Quest is provided by NomoLabs. Contact support@nomolabs.pro about this policy or your family's information.

Information used by the app

For online families we store parent account details (name, email and authentication identifiers), family membership, child profile name and age, PIN/password hashes, optional avatars, missions, submissions and optional photo proof, rewards, coins and XP history, notifications, consent choices, and any reports or blocks you create. We do not receive your Google password.

Why information is used

We use these records to authenticate family members, synchronize missions and parental reviews, maintain progress and rewards, protect accounts, and respond to safety or support requests. Google provides Google sign-in. Our hosting service processes the app database and uploaded content on our behalf.

Optional phone notifications and audio

If you enable phone notifications, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) processes a device registration token, installation identifier, opaque account/session identifiers and generic update text to deliver alerts. Private child names, mission descriptions, PINs and photo proof are not included in these push messages. Notification registrations are linked to your sign-in session; signing out or session expiry stops further server delivery for that session. Delivery metadata is retried temporarily; your in-app notification history remains with your family records. You can turn alerts off in the app or Android settings.

Custom notification audio is selected by you from a saved file and copied to the phone notification-sound collection so Android can play it when the app is closed. The app does not upload the audio or extract audio from social-media videos. These audio copies can remain on the phone after sign-out; you can remove them using the phone file manager. Sound choices are stored per account on that phone.

Children and public features

Parents manage children's profiles and approve mission rewards. Children's photo proof is available to authorized parents in their family, not public leagues. Public league participation requires the app's parental consent and opt-in controls. Public boards may show an approved nickname, avatar and ranking. Family challenges show family-level standings. Parents should avoid identifiable or sensitive photos and use a non-identifying avatar for public features.

Local mode and account mode

Device-only PIN families save progress on that device. Account-backed families save records with our service. These are separate modes; local progress is not automatically merged into an online family. Google itself does not store your Real Life Quest mission history.

Retention, access and deletion

Family records and submitted photos remain while the account is active unless deleted through available controls. Parents can export family information and request deletion. Deletion removes associated live-service records; if any information must be retained for security or legal reasons we will explain the applicable reason. For access, correction or deletion help, contact support. See account deletion.

Security and choices

Online release connections use HTTPS. Passwords/PINs are stored as hashes; access is limited by family roles. No system is guaranteed immune to security incidents. This version does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, purchases or third-party analytics SDKs. Optional photos and public features are under family controls.

Policy changes

We will update this page when data practices change. Contact support before sharing information if you have questions.
